
Project Description: Develop a sampling acceleration framework that uses a birth-death process to reduce the number of diffusion sampling steps required to generate high-quality samples. The approach should explore how birth-death dynamics can selectively add, remove or adapt sampling states while maintaining the quality and diversity of the original diffusion model.

Expected Deliverables: 1. Diffusion Sampling Baseline: Implement a standard diffusion model and sampling pipeline to establish baseline sample quality and inference time. 2. Birth-Death Sampling Framework: Develop a birth-death process-based mechanism to adaptively modify or reduce diffusion sampling steps. 3. Accelerated Sampling Method: Integrate the proposed mechanism with the diffusion sampler and demonstrate reduced sampling steps and inference time while maintaining generation quality. 4. Quality and Efficiency Evaluation: Compare the proposed method with standard diffusion sampling metrics. Ablation & Robustness Study: Analyze the effect of different birth and death strategies and sampling configurations on speed, stability and generation quality.
